Help

What is a Skill Fingerprint?

A profile of your skills where every item shows where it came from: your CV, what you wrote yourself, a colleague, a lesson, a contribution, a test or a job simulation. There is no overall score.

How do I start?

Choose Try it now, then Continue as guest or create an account. In stage 1 you add your CV and answer a few short questions, and the fingerprint appears under Discover.

What are the four stages?

1 Register / Discover: build your fingerprint. 2 Develop & Express: topics, contributions and lessons. 3 Demonstrate: apply to roles and take job simulations. 4 Connect: the employer view with anonymised profiles.

What do the coloured labels mean?

They show the origin of each piece of evidence. Anyone reading your profile can tell what you claimed yourself from what others confirmed or what was assessed.

What is the Candidate / Employer switch?

It switches between the student view and the employer view of the same demo, so you can see what a recruiter sees: anonymised mini-CVs, assessments and side-by-side comparison.

Where is my data stored?

In guest mode only in this browser. With an account it is saved to your account. Companies and sample candidates are fictional demo data.

Is there an overall score?

No. Your fingerprint never turns into a single number, and no automated hiring decision is made.
